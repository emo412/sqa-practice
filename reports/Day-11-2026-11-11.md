### Concepts covered

1. Positive testing: valid inputs and expected successful behavior.
2. Negative testing: invalid inputs and correct rejection.
3. Equivalence Partitioning: grouping inputs into valid and invalid partitions.
4. Identifying distinct invalid partitions and selecting representative values.
5. Expected results for invalid inputs, including validation messages and rejection.
6. Negative test design for missing, malformed, and out-of-range inputs.
7. Error handling and prohibited side effects: an error response does not pass if an invalid action still occurs.
8. Determining PASS/FAIL by comparing expected and actual results.
